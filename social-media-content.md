## LinkedIn 发布内容

```
After 7 days of building, we shipped ViralOS at UCWS 2026 hackathon.

It's an AI system where 5 specialized agents collaborate to generate complete marketing campaigns.

Here's the architecture insight that surprised me most:

We initially built one "marketing AI agent" that did everything.
The output was... mediocre.

Then we split it into specialized agents:
🔍 Market Analyst Agent
✍️ Content Writer Agent  
📈 Growth Optimizer Agent
🎬 Campaign Director (orchestrator)

Quality jumped dramatically.

The lesson: LLMs are better when they're focused. A specialized "viral content writer" with a narrow system prompt consistently outperforms a generalist.

We're calling this the "specialist agent" pattern. It applies well beyond marketing.

The tool is fully open source (MIT). You can self-host or use our API.

Would love feedback from marketers: does this solve a real pain point you have?

#AI #AgentPatterns #OpenSource #BuildInPublic #hackathon
```
